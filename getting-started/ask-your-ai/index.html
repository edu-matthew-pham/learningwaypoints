One link,

The Waypoints Kit is a small set of plain files. Paste a link to it into whatever AI you already use, and it reads the framework and your curriculum standard before it answers. Nothing runs on this site, and nothing you paste comes to us.

Let it ask you

If you're not sure what you want yet, paste this. The AI will ask what year, subject and standard you teach, and offer two or three things it can do.

Read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/edu-matthew-pham/waypoints-kit/main/START-HERE.md and treat it as my instructions. Complete every step in it before you answer, then ask me what I teach.
Works with any AI that can open web links (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot and most others). If yours can't, the Kit's own page explains what to paste instead.
