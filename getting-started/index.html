Not sure where to begin?

  • If you'd like to see the whole curriculum at a glance, Curriculum lays out every subject and year, with each standard broken into the steps that lead to it.
  • If you lead a subject or plan units for several classes, Plan is a good place to start. You build one unit from the curriculum's sequence, and every class works from it.
  • If you're teaching outside your subject, or new to it, you might find Lessons most useful. The tutorials come ready to use, with teacher notes written for anyone, not just specialists.
  • If none of these quite fits, Ask your AI will ask what you teach and suggest where to begin.
