Teach from the library,

Teach from our library of lessons, or make your own. Tutorials are short interactive lessons in coding, maths, science and more. Students work through them on their own, and every lesson comes with teacher notes written for anyone, not just subject specialists. Prepare turns a lesson outline into slides and printable handouts. Audio makes study episodes that follow the same sequence. Signal and Insight help you check understanding during and after the lesson. We check every lesson before it goes in and keep refining them as they're used, and teachers can request or contribute their own.

▤ TutorialsLive Open Short interactive lessons in coding, maths, science, engineering, digital systems and AI. Students work through them in the browser; teachers get plain-language notes, slides and answers. ▦ PrepareBeta Open Describe a lesson and get slides and printable handouts. Edit in the browser, present from it, or export to PowerPoint, PDF or Google Docs. ♬ AudioBeta Open Study episodes that follow your unit's sequence, one per step, made in your browser and exported as MP3s.

Formative assessment

Check understanding during and after a lesson. Signal check-ins can go straight into a Prepare deck. Both are still being tried in classrooms.

◉ SignalBeta Open Students say how confident they feel at each step, before, during or after a lesson, so you can see where every class is. No student accounts, just a class code. ◐ InsightPreview Open After a Topic Explorer session, see what students understood, what they got wrong, and which misconceptions to pick up with the whole class.

Make your own

Teachers can write a tutorial in Tutorials' Create page, or ask for one through the request form. Each one is checked before it's added to the library.
