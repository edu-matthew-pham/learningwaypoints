Plan one unit that works for all your classes. There are two ways to do it here. The Planner builds the whole unit for you from the curriculum's sequence of key ideas, so the lessons, resources, glossary, slides and handouts all line up. The Kit works in the AI you already use, so you can start from your own plan, or ask how to spread a standard over your lessons and where a misunderstanding or a thin foundation could cause trouble later. Both work from the same waypoints. When classes move at different speeds, either way changes the width rather than the pace. A faster class does more with each idea, with richer examples and connections, instead of racing on to the next one.
